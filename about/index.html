morphos_ui

A rendering engine for Flutter: thousands of cards on one spring-animated surface. Grids morph into lists, cards glide into place, and it only draws what is on screen and only animates what is moving. Tested on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux and web.

► Live demo How it works
  • Levelwith a hand-written CustomPainter at 5,000 cards, with hit testing, accessibility and keyboard support built in
  • ~8× fasterthan a hand-written painter when 100,000 cards morph at once
  • ~33× fasterwhen one card changes in a 100,000-card list
