Live demos

The example app of each package, compiled to Flutter web and running in your browser. No install, nothing to read first. Back to the packages.

  • scroll_transition
    Twelve scroll effects — fade, scale, 3D drum, blur, carousel, pinned headers. Open one and scroll.
    pub.dev
  • middle_click_autoscroll
    Middle-click the list and move the mouse: browser-style autoscroll, with the mode, dead zone and speed curve exposed.
    pub.dev
  • universal_spell_check
    Type a misspelling and get it underlined, with suggestions, a bundled Hunspell dictionary and custom word lists.
    pub.dev
  • material_defaults
    Every Material 3 default style Flutter keeps private, component by component, live for the theme you pick.
    pub.dev
  • web_update_guard
    A stamped build detecting a new deploy. The page can simulate a redeploy so you can watch the guard react.
    pub.dev
  • input_language
    Keyboard-language detection and automatic text direction — on the web through the browser-locale fallback.
    pub.dev
  • mobius_carousel
    A drag-to-claim offer carousel: cards tilt into place, the centre card pulls down to reveal the reward.
    pub.dev
